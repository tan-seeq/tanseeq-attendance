<product_requirements>
The TANSEEQ HR application is a bilingual system for HR management, addressing several key areas:
1.  **Work Reports + Clients Master:** Initially featured AES-256-GCM encryption and RBAC, later simplified to plain-text passwords and universal access.
2.  **Notification System:** Allows Super Admins to send private notifications and employees to receive status updates.
3.  **Attendance Policy (Previous):** Penalized only early clock-out (before 6 PM); no penalties for early clock-in.
4.  **Payroll & Reporting:** Corrected payroll logic and generated bilingual PDF reports.
5.  **Marketing/Client Visits:** Enables employees to log visits with start/end times, client details, location, and purpose. Requires mandatory reports, automatic duration calculation, Super Admin notifications, and prevents concurrent visits, with optional GPS support.
6.  **Advances and Loans:** Manages employee advances/loans, including invoice attachments, automatic deduction (revised to distinguish advances from custody), and remaining balance display for Super Admins, with full CRUD operations and deduction source control.
7.  **New Attendance Policies (October 2025):** Introduces advanced deduction rules for lateness (free occurrences, cumulative minutes, half/full-day), early leave, and missing check-outs, with specific exceptions for Hatem (no penalties) and Tareq (flexible end time).
8.  **Enhanced Notifications:** Automatic notifications for attendance issues, deductions, and Super Admin actions. A mandatory in-app modal for unread notifications is required.
9.  **Expanded Super Admin Powers:** Full CRUD (create, edit, delete with soft delete/audit log) for manual deductions and comprehensive control (edit, adjust, attach, close, void) over Advances/Loans after approval, supported by robust RBAC and audit logging.
</product_requirements>
