<product_requirements>
The application, TANSEEQ HR, is a comprehensive, bilingual (Arabic/English) HR management system. The core system, built on MongoDB, manages employees, attendance, leave, and payroll.

A key feature, Daily Work Report + Clients Master, was developed to track client-related work. Initially, this module required a robust, secure system for managing sensitive client credentials, featuring AES-256-GCM encryption, role-based access control (RBAC), and a dedicated UI for administrators to manage employee permissions.

However, following implementation and user feedback, a major architectural change was requested: the complete removal of the encryption and RBAC systems. The current requirement is that all users have full, unrestricted permissions to view/manage all data, and all passwords are to be stored in plain text.

Most recently, development has shifted to three new requirements:
1.  **Notification System:** A feature for Super Admins to send private notifications to specific employees.
2.  **Attendance Policy Change:** No penalties for clocking in before 9 AM; penalties apply only for clocking out before 6 PM.
3.  **Payroll & Reporting Overhaul:** A complete fix of the payroll calculation logic and a professional redesign of PDF reports for A4 printing with improved bilingual support.
</product_requirements>
