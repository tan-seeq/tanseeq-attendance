<product_requirements>
The TANSEEQ HR application is a bilingual system for HR management. Key requirements:
1.  **Work Reports + Clients Master:** Originally had AES-256-GCM encryption and RBAC, later reversed to plain-text passwords and unrestricted access for all users.
2.  **Notification System:** Super Admins send private notifications; employees receive status updates on requests.
3.  **Attendance Policy (Previous):** No penalties for early clock-in; penalties only for early clock-out (before 6 PM).
4.  **Payroll & Reporting:** Fixed incorrect payroll logic and redesigned bilingual PDF reports.
5.  **Marketing/Client Visits:** System for employees to log visits (start/end times, client details, location, purpose), requiring mandatory reports, automatic duration, Super Admin notifications, and preventing concurrent visits. Optional GPS support.
6.  **Advances and Loans:** System to manage employee advances/loans, allowing invoice attachments, automatic deduction (initially, then revised), and displaying remaining balance to Super Admins. This feature was significantly refined to distinguish between advances (settled against salary) and custody (deducted from expenses), with Super Admin control over deduction source and full CRUD operations.
7.  **New Attendance Policies (October 2025):** Advanced deduction rules for lateness (free occurrences, cumulative minutes, half/full-day deductions), early leave, and missing check-outs. Specific exceptions for Hatem (no penalties) and Tareq (flexible end time).
8.  **Enhanced Notifications:** Automatic notifications for attendance issues, deductions, and Super Admin actions. Mandatory in-app modal display for unread notifications.
9.  **Expanded Super Admin Powers:** Full CRUD (create, edit, delete with soft delete/audit log) for manual deductions and full control (edit, adjust, attach, close, void) over Advances/Loans after approval, with robust RBAC and audit logging.
</product_requirements>
