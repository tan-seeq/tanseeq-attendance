<product_requirements>
The TANSEEQ HR application is a bilingual (Arabic/English) system for HR management, covering employees, attendance, leave, and payroll. Key requirements evolved:
1.  **Work Reports + Clients Master:** Initially required secure, AES-256-GCM encrypted client credentials and role-based access control (RBAC). This was later reversed; all users now require full, unrestricted access, and passwords are to be stored in plain text.
2.  **Notification System:** Super Admins need to send private notifications/warnings to specific employees. Employees should receive approval/rejection notifications for leave/field exit requests.
3.  **Attendance Policy:** No penalties for clocking in before 9 AM; penalties apply only for clocking out before 6 PM.
4.  **Payroll & Reporting Overhaul:** Fix incorrect payroll calculation logic, and professionally redesign PDF reports for A4 printing with improved bilingual support.
5.  **Marketing/Client Visits:** A system for employees to record client visits, including start/end times (server-generated), client details, location, and purpose. It requires a mandatory report at visit conclusion, automatic duration calculation, Super Admin notifications upon completion, prevention of concurrent visits, and optional GPS support.
6.  **Advances and Loans:** A system to manage employee advances or loans, allowing attachment of invoices, automatic deduction of invoice amounts, and displaying the remaining loan balance to Super Admins.
</product_requirements>
